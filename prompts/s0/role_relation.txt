ROLE: relation
Focus on how concepts relate: causal or functional relations, dependencies, impact of X on Y,
conditions under which X improves Y, mappings between task settings and methods.

Examples:
1. How does the choice of uncertainty estimator influence the effectiveness of an acquisition function in DAL?
2. What is the relationship between dataset shift (domain drift) and acquisition strategy performance?
3. How does label noise affect stopping criteria and sample selection in deep active learning?
