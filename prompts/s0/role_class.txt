ROLE: class
Focus on what exists in the domain: entities, concepts, artifacts, datasets, model families,
uncertainty estimators, acquisition functions, annotation units, and evaluation objects.
Ask about required classes and taxonomies that the ontology must represent.

Examples:
1. What types of acquisition functions are used in deep active learning, and how are they categorized?
2. What forms of uncertainty estimation are commonly used in DAL (e.g., Bayesian, ensembles), and what are their main variants?
3. What annotation units exist in DAL for NLP tasks (token, span, sentence, document), and how do they relate to labeling cost?
