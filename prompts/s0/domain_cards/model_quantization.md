Domain: **Model Quantization** (model_quantization). Model quantization compresses and accelerates neural networks by representing weights and/or activations in lower precision (e.g., **INT8/INT4/FP8**) while controlling accuracy degradation. Core elements include quantization granularity (per-tensor / per-channel / group-wise), quantization targets (**weight-only** vs **weight–activation**, and in LLMs often KV-cache-aware variants), calibration data and scaling/zero-point strategies, handling outliers and dynamic range (clipping, smoothing/redistribution of activation outliers), and two primary regimes: **post-training quantization (PTQ)** with minimal or no retraining, and **quantization-aware training (QAT)** that simulates quantization during training to reduce error. A typical loop selects a bit-width and scheme under deployment constraints (latency/memory/energy and hardware support), calibrates and quantizes (PTQ) or fine-tunes with quantization effects (QAT), exports to a deployment stack, and benchmarks both quality and efficiency; evaluation emphasizes quality retention (task metrics / perplexity), stability across inputs, and **system trade-offs** (memory footprint, throughput, tail latency, energy/cost per request).
