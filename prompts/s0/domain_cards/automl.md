Domain: **Automated Machine Learning (automl)**. AutoML targets progressive automation of the end-to-end ML pipeline—data preprocessing/cleaning, feature engineering, model/algorithm selection, hyperparameter optimization (HPO), and neural architecture search (NAS)—so that competitive models can be produced under explicit budgets and constraints with reduced expert intervention. Core elements include (i) **structured search spaces** over pipelines, hyperparameters, and architectures; (ii) **optimization engines** (e.g., Bayesian optimization, evolutionary methods, reinforcement learning, local/continuous optimization); (iii) **meta-learning / transfer** for warm-starting and cross-dataset generalization; (iv) **multi-fidelity evaluation** (early stopping, subsampling, surrogate modeling/performance prediction) to reduce search cost; and (v) **selection/ensembling** and postprocessing for robust final performance. A typical loop defines an objective and constraints (time/compute, memory/latency, sometimes multi-objective trade-offs), iteratively proposes candidate configurations, evaluates them via validation or cross-validation, updates the search strategy (surrogate/acquisition or evolutionary operators), allocates additional budget to promising candidates, and finalizes by refitting and (optionally) ensembling; evaluation emphasizes best-achieved quality **vs. budget**, time-to-quality, stability across datasets, and cost/efficiency of the search procedure.
