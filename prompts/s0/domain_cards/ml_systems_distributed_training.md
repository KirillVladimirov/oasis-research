Domain: **ml_systems_distributed_training**. This domain studies the systems and runtime mechanisms that scale ML training across many accelerators and nodes while preserving convergence and cost-efficiency under heterogeneous, dynamic cluster conditions. Core elements include parallelism strategies (**data**, **tensor/model**, **pipeline**, and hybrid/"N-D" schemes), automatic parallel plan generation and reconfiguration, topology-aware **collective communication** (all-reduce/all-gather/reduce-scatter) with computation–communication overlap, memory optimizations (parameter/optimizer sharding, activation rematerialization/checkpointing), straggler and workload-imbalance mitigation (variable sequence lengths, heterogeneous GPUs), elasticity and geo-distribution, and operational concerns such as checkpointing and fault tolerance. A typical loop profiles compute/communication and memory pressure for a target model, selects or auto-derives a parallelism plan and schedule, deploys and monitors goodput (tokens/sec or step time) and stability, then iterates on batch/microbatch sizing, placement, overlap, and resilience policies; evaluation emphasizes **scaling efficiency**, time-to-quality/time-to-train, communication volume, GPU utilization, and cost under realistic heterogeneity and failures.
