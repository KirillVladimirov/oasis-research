Domain: **Multimodal AI (multimodal_ai)**. Multimodal AI develops models that **jointly learn and reason across multiple modalities**—most commonly text–image, and increasingly video, audio, and structured signals—by aligning representations and enabling cross-modal understanding and generation. Core elements include **multimodal representation alignment** (notably contrastive vision–language pretraining in the CLIP family), fusion/interaction mechanisms (late fusion, cross-attention, modality adapters), and multimodal generative modeling for captioning, VQA, grounding, and instruction-following **multimodal LLMs** via visual instruction tuning. The domain also covers retrieval-augmented multimodal pipelines (multimodal RAG for VQA/knowledge-intensive tasks), robustness issues such as modality bias and hallucination/grounding failures, and continual/domain adaptation for new visual domains. A typical loop curates paired (and optionally unpaired) multimodal corpora, pretrains with contrastive and/or generative objectives, aligns capabilities via instruction tuning and task finetuning, and evaluates on retrieval, VQA/captioning, grounding, and video understanding benchmarks—tracking both task quality and generalization/robustness (including zero-shot transfer and failure-mode analysis).
