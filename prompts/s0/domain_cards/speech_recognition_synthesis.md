Domain: **Speech Recognition & Speech Synthesis (speech_recognition_synthesis)**. This domain covers building systems that map **speech → text** (ASR) and **text → speech/audio** (TTS / text-to-audio), plus adjacent identity and transformation tasks (speaker verification, voice conversion) and language modeling for speech pipelines. Core elements include speech representations (log-mel/MFCC-style features), acoustic modeling and sequence learning (CTC, attention, transducer-style decoding), streaming/low-latency recognition, speaker and prosody control, neural vocoders and waveform generation, multilingual/low-resource regimes, robustness to noise/channel variability, and confidence estimation for production use. A typical loop curates and normalizes speech–text (or speech-only / text-only) corpora, trains an encoder–decoder or transducer/vocoder stack, tunes decoding and latency constraints, and iterates with error analysis (substitutions/insertions/deletions; prosody artifacts); evaluation emphasizes recognition error (WER/CER), synthesis naturalness and speaker similarity (often MOS-style or learned proxies), and systems metrics such as real-time factor, tail latency, and robustness under domain shift.
