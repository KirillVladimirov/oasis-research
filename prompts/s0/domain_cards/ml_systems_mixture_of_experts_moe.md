Domain: **ml_systems_mixture_of_experts_moe**. This domain studies the **systems bottlenecks and optimizations** required to train and serve **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)** models, where conditional computation activates only a small subset of experts per token but introduces heavy **routing/dispatch** and **many-to-many communication** overheads. Core elements include router/gating execution (top-k routing, capacity factors), **load-balancing and expert utilization** control (auxiliary losses and routing constraints), token dispatch/combine pipelines (often dominated by **all-to-all** collectives), expert parallelism and hybrid parallel strategies (tensor–expert–data combinations), memory pressure from expert weights and activation/KV state, and low-level performance engineering (kernel fusion/persistent kernels, overlap of communication with expert compute, straggler mitigation and padding avoidance). A typical loop instruments the MoE forward/backward pass to quantify routing and communication costs, selects a parallelization/placement plan for experts and shared blocks, applies dispatch and kernel/collective optimizations, and re-evaluates; evaluation emphasizes goodput (tokens/sec), scaling efficiency, GPU utilization, tail latency/stragglers, communication volume, and cost-to-quality when increasing expert count or sparsity.
