Domain: **Healthcare Foundation Models** (healthcare_foundation_models). This domain concerns **large pre-trained (foundation) models** adapted to medical use cases across heterogeneous clinical modalities and settings. Core elements include modality-specific backbones—**clinical LLMs** for notes/EHR and medical knowledge, **vision foundation models** for radiology and digital pathology, **audio models** for signals such as heart/lung sounds, and **multimodal models** that align text–image–tabular signals for decision support and summarization. Key technical concerns span pretraining on sensitive, weakly labeled clinical corpora; adaptation via prompting, instruction tuning, parameter-efficient finetuning, and retrieval augmentation; and reliability constraints: dataset shift across hospitals, calibration and uncertainty, robustness to missing/noisy documentation, and clinically meaningful evaluation beyond narrow benchmarks (including safety, bias, and operational utility). A typical loop curates modality-aligned corpora (often multi-institutional), pretrains/self-supervises at scale, aligns the model to clinical tasks with preference/instruction data (and/or structured supervision), validates with domain-specific protocols and real-world endpoints, and deploys with monitoring and governance; evaluation emphasizes clinical validity and utility, robustness across sites, and failure-mode characterization alongside standard performance metrics.
